About

Who I am

I am a native Peruvian molecular biologist and postdoctoral scientist at KU Leuven's Department of Microbiology and Immunology, dedicated to elucidating the role of human microbial communities in health and disease.

My work bridges rigorous microbiome science with fieldwork in underrepresented populations, including a 15-year collaborative study with the Matsés people of the Peruvian Amazon — an effort built on long-term trust, ethical co-design, and community partnership.

Beyond the bench, I lead research programs in the Global South, working to ensure that microbiome science reflects the full diversity of humanity.

Research

Projects

Ongoing · Amazon

Matsés Microbiome Study

A 15-year community-based collaborative study investigating the gut microbiome of the Matsés, an Indigenous Amazonian people. Developed an ethical participatory framework using community assemblies and co-designed protocols, now proposed as a model for Indigenous microbiome research globally.

Clinical · Belgium

Microbiome & Colorectal Cancer

Investigating microbial confounders in colorectal cancer development, including landmark work published in Nature Medicine. Identifying bacterial signatures associated with early-stage disease and evaluating their diagnostic potential.

Immunology · Belgium

Gut Microbiome in Rheumatoid Arthritis

Characterizing gut microbiome shifts in preclinical rheumatoid arthritis patients at KU Leuven, contributing to the understanding of how dysbiosis may precede and drive systemic autoimmune conditions.

Virology · Bioinformatics

Human Gut Virome

Profiling the human gut virome across diverse populations using metagenomic approaches. Exploring viral diversity, phage-bacteria interactions, and how virome composition relates to health outcomes in both Western and non-Western cohorts.

Ancient DNA · Archaeology

Ancient Microbiomes

Reconstructing the microbial communities of ancient human populations from archaeological samples. Combining aDNA techniques with modern metagenomics to trace the co-evolution of humans and their microbiota across millennia.
