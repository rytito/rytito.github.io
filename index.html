Raul Y. Tito Tadeo

Microbiome & parasite genomics

I study the multi-domain gut microbiome — bacteria and their eukaryotic neighbours — across populations, time, and the urbanization continuum. Dual doctorate: Biomedical Sciences (KU Leuven) and Bio-Engineering Sciences (Vrije Universiteit Brussel). Much of my current work centres on Blastocystis, the most common gut eukaryote in humans, and on making the public sequence data our field depends on trustworthy.

Current focus

Curating the public Blastocystis record

An open pipeline that audits every Blastocystis SSU rRNA sequence on GenBank — validating subtype labels, placing unlabelled deposits, and surfacing candidate novel lineages (including a chelonian lineage) with a full provenance trail for every record.

Code & curated reference set · Presented at the 5th International Blastocystis Conference, La Laguna, September 2026
